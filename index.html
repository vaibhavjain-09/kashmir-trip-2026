06

Weather & caveats

Read this section again the week before flying.

Doodhpathri temporarily closed

Tourists have been asked to avoid the Doodhpathri–Yusmarg forest area until further orders after an ongoing security operation in Budgam (early September). The main circuit — Srinagar, Gulmarg, Pahalgam, Sonamarg — is not affected. Oct 11 defaults to Srinagar sightseeing with a free swap if it reopens.

Sonamarg is tail-season in mid-October

Hotels wind down and the Zojila road can close with early snow. Reconfirm the hotel and road status directly before the Gulmarg → Sonamarg leg, and treat Zero Point as a same-morning decision.

Security watch is active

The situation is being checked daily against this route. Anything that could affect the planned areas, roads or activities gets flagged as it appears.

Cold and early dark

Expect cold mornings and evenings, light fading by ~6 PM, and possible snow at Phase 2 and Zero Point. The plan wraps outdoor time by ~5:15 PM. Pack layers, child thermals, gloves, sunscreen and motion-sickness basics for the mountain roads.

Altitude on Gondola Phase 2

Phase 2 reaches ~3,980 m. Plan is Phase 1 + Phase 2, with child eligibility verified at booking and a same-day weather check before riding up.
